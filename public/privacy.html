Privacy notice

Last updated 14 October 2026

Who we are

Strong Suit (strongsuitgame.com) is a daily quiz game run by Jack Howse, a sole trader in the UK. Jack is the "controller" of your personal data. You can contact us about anything in this notice at hello@strongsuitgame.com.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you first open the game we create a guest account, identified only by a random ID. If you choose to sign in, we also hold the email address you sign in with. If you sign in with Google, Google's sign-in service records the name and photo on your Google account as part of your sign-in record. We never use or show them.
  • Your nickname. We give you one like "Player 4821". You can change it.
  • How you play. For each hand: how you ranked the categories, the answers you chose, how long you took, your scores, and the stats worked out from them (such as streaks and the subjects you're strongest in). We also keep the groups you join.

We don't show ads, we don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use analytics tools. The game stores a small amount of information in your browser to keep you signed in and to remember settings, such as whether you've seen the How to play guide. That storage is needed for the game to work.

Why we use it

  • To run the game you've asked to play: keeping your scores, stats, groups and sign-in working ("contract").
  • To keep the game fair and working well: preventing cheating, fixing problems, and using combined, anonymous figures (such as how often a category is skipped) to choose better questions ("legitimate interests").

Who can see it

  • Other players see your nickname and daily score on the leaderboard. Members of your groups see your nickname, your scores, how you ranked each hand, the answers you chose (only once they've finished that hand themselves), and your stats for each subject, such as your average and how often you skip it.
  • Google provides our hosting, database, sign-in and server code through Firebase. Our database and server code are in London. Google's sign-in service may process data outside the UK, including in the US, under the UK's approved safeguards for international transfers.
  • Cloudflare manages our domain name and forwards emails sent to our address.

We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and play history: until you delete your account. We may delete guest accounts that haven't been used for 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, object to how we use it, or ask us to delete it. You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time from your profile in the game ("Delete my account and data"). For anything else, email hello@strongsuitgame.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Strong Suit is for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects you significantly, we'll tell you in the game.
