How to play Strong Suit

Strong Suit is a daily trivia quiz. Everyone gets the same hand each day, and the twist is that you decide which categories count for the most.

The rules

  1. Each day's hand has five categories, each from a different subject: film and TV, music, sport, geography and so on.
  2. Tap them in order from strongest to weakest. Your weakest one is skipped.
  3. You play the other four weakest first, and each round is worth more than the last:
    • ×1
    • ×2
    • ×3
    • ×4
  4. Each round is six pairs to match, such as capital cities to countries. Every answer belongs to exactly one prompt, and you submit all six at once.
  5. Each correct pair scores that round's multiplier, so a perfect hand is 60 points.

There's no time limit. A small stopwatch runs during each round, but it only matters if you tie with someone on points.

After the hand

Self-awareness compares your order with the best order you could have picked, given how you actually did in each round. Put your best round at ×4 and your worst at ×1 and you'll score 100%.

The bonus round lets you play the category you skipped. It doesn't count towards your score, but it does tell you whether skipping it was the right call.

Then there's a share card, which shows how you did without giving away any answers.

FAQ

When does a new hand come out?
At midnight where you are. It's the same numbered hand for everyone, so someone in Sydney plays it hours before someone in London.
Is it free?
Yes. The daily hand is free and always will be, and there are no ads. ♠ Plus is coming later with extras like an archive of past hands and trends over time, but it will only ever add things. Nothing free will move behind it.
Do I need an account?
Not to play. You can play straight away as a guest. To join or start a group, or to appear on the global leaderboard, create a free account with Google or an emailed link. It also keeps your scores if you change phone or clear your browser, and the hands you've played as a guest carry over.
Can I play with friends?
Yes. Start a group, share the invite code, and you'll get daily, monthly and all-time tables, plus a look at how everyone ranked the hand once you've played it yourself. There's also a global leaderboard. Both need a free account.
What subjects come up?
Film and TV, music, sport, geography, history, science and nature, food and drink, language, art and literature, and pop culture. Every so often a 🃏 Joker round turns up for something sillier.
Can I play a hand I missed?
Not yet. You can see every hand you've played, but missed hands will come back with the ♠ Plus archive.
Can I practise first?
Yes. Tap the ? on the home screen, or go to your profile, for a practice hand. It isn't timed and doesn't count.
Can I look the answers up?
Technically, yes. There's no timer to stop you. But the leaderboard is less fun if you do, and you'll learn less about your strong suit.
Who makes it?
Jack Howse, in the UK. Questions, corrections and complaints about a round all go to hello@strongsuitgame.com.
