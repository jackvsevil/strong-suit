How to play

  1. You're dealt five categories. Tap them from strongest to weakest. You'll skip your weakest!

  2. You play the other four from weakest to strongest, and each round is worth more than the last.

    ×1×2×3×4

  3. Each round, match six pairs in 30 seconds. Every correct pair scores its round's multiplier.

  4. The best possible score is 60. Your self-awareness shows how close your order was to the best order for your results.
